I'm Ulla Santti, an RDI specialist and project manager in bio- and circular economy research at Savonia University of Applied Sciences. In my work I build solutions that strengthen our region's vitality, sustainable growth, and new jobs. In my free time I'm working on my doctoral thesis and enjoy playing badminton. I believe wellbeing comes from balancing work, family, and everyday life.

I want to build a safer, more vibrant Savo-Karelia, where people get timely access to care, local services are preserved, and people get help when they need it. Bio- and circular economy, entrepreneurship, and innovation can bring our region work and hope for the future. At the same time, we must take care of clean waters, nature, and domestic security of supply.
