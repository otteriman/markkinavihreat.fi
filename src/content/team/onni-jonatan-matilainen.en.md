Onni-Jonatan Matilainen is a former deputy city councillor from Oulu, a deputy member of the party council, and a master's student in economics. Matilainen currently works at the OECD on international tax policy. His key political themes include, among others, sustainable development, climate policy, industrial policy, and reformist social policy.
