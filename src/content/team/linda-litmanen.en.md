I'm Linda Litmanen, an experienced occupational health psychologist and psychotherapist from Pirkkala, and a mother of two. Through my work I have gained a broad understanding of how different organisations and the various levels of society work.

In my home municipality of Pirkkala I serve as first vice-chair of the municipal council and as Pirkkala's representative on the regional board of the Tampere city region. I am also currently a staff representative on the boards of two limited companies at my workplace.

My values as a political actor rest on the idea that today's decisions must make a good life possible here and now, and safeguard the conditions for it for future generations too. That is why I put children first in decision-making.

The balance between social, ecological and economic sustainability must be maintained, taking into account the sustainability of the population as well.
